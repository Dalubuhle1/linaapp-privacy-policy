Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Linamandla ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the LINA HRMS Android application and related services.

LINA HRMS is an employee management application designed to support employee administration, attendance tracking, leave management, payroll processing and human resource operations.

We are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with applicable South African data protection legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Important: LINA HRMS is intended for employees and authorized administrators. Personal information is processed primarily for legitimate employment and human resource management purposes.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Login Information

When you access the application, we may process:

  • Employee email address.
  • Employee identification and account information.
  • Login credentials.
  • Account status and assigned user role.
  • Device identification information, including IMEI where collected and supported.
  • Authentication and account activity information.

2.2 Employee Information

The application may process employment-related information, including:

  • Employee names and contact details.
  • Employment information and joining dates.
  • Employee profiles.
  • Education and qualifications.
  • Previous employment and work experience.
  • Employee roles and employment status.

2.3 Location Information

LINA HRMS may access GPS or device location information when location-based attendance functionality is used and the necessary Android permissions have been granted.

Location information may be used for:

  • Attendance tracking.
  • Clock-in and clock-out verification.
  • Workplace attendance administration.
  • Supporting employee attendance records.

Location access depends on the functionality implemented in the application and the permissions granted by the user. You can manage location permissions through Android settings. Disabling location access may affect attendance features.

2.4 Attendance Information

We may collect and process:

  • Clock-in and clock-out records.
  • Attendance dates and times.
  • Working hours.
  • Overtime records.
  • Attendance history.
  • Location information associated with attendance, where applicable.

2.5 Payroll and Salary Information

The application may process confidential payroll information:

  • Employee salary and hourly pay rates.
  • Regular earnings.
  • Overtime earnings.
  • Payroll periods.
  • Payslip information.
  • Applicable deductions, including UIF.
  • Net salary calculations.

2.6 Leave Information

Where leave management features are enabled, information may include:

  • Leave applications and requested dates.
  • Sick leave and annual leave information.
  • Leave reasons.
  • Leave balances and accruals.
  • Leave approval and rejection records.
  • Leave cancellation information.
  • Leave-related payroll deductions.

2.7 Employee Notes

The application includes an employee notes feature. Notes may be stored locally on the Android device using the application's offline Room database.

Local notes are separate from the application's authentication storage. Their availability depends on the device and application installation.

2.8 Technical Information

We may process technical information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Device and Android operating system information.
  • API requests and server connection information.
  • Application diagnostics.
  • Authentication and security logs.

3. Android Permissions

Certain Android permissions may be necessary to provide application functionality.

Location Permission

Location permission supports GPS-based attendance functionality where enabled. You can manage or revoke this permission through your device settings.

We do not represent that the application continuously tracks your location. Collection depends on the implemented functionality and permissions granted.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate employees and administrators.
  • Manage employee accounts and access permissions.
  • Maintain employee profiles.
  • Record attendance and working hours.
  • Support location-based attendance verification.
  • Calculate salaries and overtime.
  • Generate employee payslips.
  • Manage leave applications and balances.
  • Support human resource administration.
  • Provide technical support.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal information is processed where a lawful basis exists under applicable data protection legislation, including POPIA.

This may include:

  • Consent, where applicable.
  • Performance of employment or contractual obligations.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate interests, where legally permitted.
  • Other lawful grounds recognized by applicable legislation.

6. How Information Is Stored

6.1 Backend Server

The Android application communicates with our backend through the following service:

Website: https://linaapp.com

The backend uses PHP, the CodeIgniter framework and a MySQL database to manage employee and application information.

Information submitted through the application may be stored on our backend infrastructure for authorized human resource and administrative operations.

6.2 Local Device Storage

Certain information may be stored locally on the Android device, including:

  • Authentication and session preferences.
  • Employee notes stored using Room database.
  • Temporary application information.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may remove locally stored information. This does not automatically delete information stored on our server.

We do not make employee personal information publicly available. Information may be shared where necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

7.1 Authorized Administrators

Authorized Linamandla administrators and HR personnel may access relevant employee information according to their assigned responsibilities and permissions.

7.2 Technical Service Providers

We may use hosting and technical service providers to maintain and operate the application and its backend infrastructure.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and subject to appropriate safeguards.

7.3 Legal Authorities

Information may be disclosed when required by law, court order or a lawful request from a regulatory or government authority.

7.4 Business Transfers

Information may be transferred as part of a legitimate merger, restructuring or business transfer where permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for employment administration, attendance, payroll, legal compliance and other legitimate purposes.

Retention periods may differ according to the type of information and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps will be taken to securely delete, destroy or anonymize it.

9. Information Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorized access.
  • Accidental loss or destruction.
  • Unauthorized modification.
  • Disclosure or misuse.
  • Unlawful processing.

The application communicates with its production backend using HTTPS to help protect information transmitted over the internet.

Access to employee information is intended to be controlled through authentication and assigned user permissions.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Our infrastructure or service providers may operate in different geographical locations. Personal information may therefore be processed or stored outside South Africa.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure international transfers comply with POPIA and other applicable data protection requirements.
